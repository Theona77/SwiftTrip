# SwiftTrip Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 9 October 2026_

SwiftTrip helps you keep your travel documents in one place: passports, ID cards, visas, boarding passes, train tickets, hotel bookings and event tickets. This policy explains what happens to the information in those documents.

## Summary

**Your documents stay on your device.** SwiftTrip has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

## What the app processes

When you scan or import a document, SwiftTrip reads it **on your iPhone**. This covers:

- **Images and PDFs** that you take with the camera or choose from Photos or Files.
- **Text in those documents.** The app reads it with Apple's on-device text recognition. For PDFs that already contain text, it reads that text directly.
- **Document type and details.** The app works out the document type and fields such as name, document number, dates, flight or booking codes. It does this with rules that run on your device, and optionally with Apple Intelligence's on-device model on supported devices. Apple Intelligence processing also stays on the device.

All of this happens locally. Nothing is uploaded.

## Where data is stored

- **Documents, extracted details and trips** are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
- **The home-screen widget** gets one small preview image and the name of your next document. These are kept in a storage area that only SwiftTrip and its widget can access.
- **iCloud or iTunes backups:** if you've turned them on for your device, your iPhone may include this data in them. Those backups are controlled by your Apple account settings, not by us.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** used only to photograph documents when you choose "Take Picture".
- **Photos and Files:** SwiftTrip only receives the specific items you pick in the system picker. It cannot browse your library.

## Deleting your data

- **Single documents:** delete them in the app. This removes the image, PDF and details.
- **Everything:** deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

SwiftTrip does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
